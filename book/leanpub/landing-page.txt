ABOUT YOUR BOOK
Shown prominently on the book landing page. Plain text.
=============================================================================

Elements of Data Science is an introduction to data science for people with no programming experience. It presents a small, powerful subset of Python that lets you do real work with data as quickly as possible: choosing questions, finding and cleaning data, exploring and visualizing it, modeling it, and communicating what you find.

You don't need to know anything about programming, statistics, or data science, and there is little math beyond arithmetic and logarithms. When the book uses a term, it defines it; when it uses a programming feature, it explains it.

The book has five parts:

* From Python to Pandas: variables, strings, dates and locations, lists and NumPy arrays, loops and files, dictionaries, and plotting
* Exploratory Data Analysis: DataFrames, distributions, relationships between variables, and linear and logistic regression
* Statistical Inference: resampling, bootstrapping, and hypothesis testing, using computation instead of mathematical analysis
* Case Study: Political Alignment, using fifty years of General Social Survey data on polarization and changing beliefs
* Case Study: Algorithmic Fairness, on COMPAS, an algorithm used in the criminal justice system, and why fairness is hard to define

Every chapter is also a Jupyter notebook you can run on Colab, with no software to install, from https://allendowney.github.io/ElementsOfDataScience. The notebooks contain the code from the book and exercises to practice what you learn.

Two editions are available in PDF and EPUB:

* Free Sample: the preface and Chapters 6 and 7
* Complete Book: all 17 chapters

The complete book is pay what you want, from $9, with a suggested price of $29. Paying for it supports free, open educational materials from Green Tea Press.

A printed edition, with a full-color interior, is available from Lulu: https://www.lulu.com/shop/allen-downey/elements-of-data-science/paperback/product-9dyrwn.html
